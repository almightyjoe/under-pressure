How it works · REV 1.7

Pick the test length (5, 10, 15, 30 min or custom) and the reading interval, then type each pressure into the next box as it comes off the gauge. Enter or ↓ goes to the next box, ↑ back. New rows appear as you type, so you can keep entering data every minute for hours. Everything is saved in the browser as you type.

You do not have to fill every box. Skip any minute you missed: all rates use the real time between the readings you did enter. Rows marked * show a rate that spans skipped readings.

Loading data from a file

Drag a CSV or text file anywhere onto the page, use 📂 Open file, or paste rows (Paste data, or just Ctrl+V). A preview shows what was found before anything is loaded: the pressure column, the time range, and the reading interval. Both comma-separated and tab-separated rows work, with or without quotes and a header row. The file can end in .csv, .txt or have no extension at all: the contents are what matter. Dates like 03.11.2026 are read day.month.year. If readings are already entered you choose Replace, Merge by time or Append. When a test log keeps growing, drop the same file again and choose Merge: only the new rows are added, with a count of what was new, updated and already present.

How the result is decided

A test period is N minutes long (the test length: 15 by default). The result is always for one period, shown shaded in the list and chart:

  • Best N min (default): the program tries every N-minute period in the data, including a test log that has the pressure build-up and bleed-off in it, and judges the period that meets the criteria best. It tells you how many periods passed.
  • Latest N min: the most recent N minutes.
  • Custom range: drag across the chart (the numbers for the selection update live below the chart as you drag), type From and To in minutes, or click a row's time (Shift+click for the end). Times snap to the nearest reading.

PASS means the period meets all of these

  • Pressure held: every reading is at or above the minimum test pressure and no more than 5% above it (a 5,000 test → 5,000 to 5,250; an 8,000 test → 8,000 to 8,400). Set the minimum test pressure at the top (4,500, 5,000, 7,500, 8,000, 10,000 or any number you type). The maximum is the minimum plus 5% unless you type an exact maximum in Settings. Leave the minimum blank to switch this check off.
  • Loss rate: under the limit (5 psi/min) over the last 5 minutes of the period.
  • Total loss: no more than the maximum for that test length (Settings; 75 psi for 15 min).
  • Loss trend: the loss per minute is not getting worse from the early half of the period to the recent half (plus a small tolerance). A rise in pressure counts as zero loss and is never a failure.

After the period the program also notes whether the pressure was bled back down to 0. That note does not change PASS or FAIL.

Reading the chart

Move the mouse over the chart to see the time and pressure of the nearest reading. Drag across it to select any part of the test: the duration, start / end / lowest / highest pressure, total loss, loss rates and the pass/fail checks for that selection are calculated as you drag and shown under the chart. Letting go makes it the judged period.

Numbers shown

  • Whole window: (start − end) ÷ minutes for the period. Last 1 / 5 / 10 min: rolling loss rate ending at the end of the period.
  • Last/min and Avg/min on each row: loss over that interval, and the average since the period started.
  • Loss per minute, reading to reading: the profile, for example 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 2.
